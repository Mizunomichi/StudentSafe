🛡️ StudentSafe

GPS-Based Safety Reporting for Students

How It Works

📍

Step 1

Open the app and allow location access. Your position is shown on the map.

⚠️

Step 2

Click "Report Danger" and select the location on the map where the incident occurred.

📝

Step 3

Fill in details: incident type, severity level, and description.

🚀

Step 4

Submit the report. It appears instantly on the map for all users to see.

Key Features

📍 Real-time GPS

Automatic location detection and tracking. See your position on the map in real-time.

🗺️ Interactive Map

Built with Leaflet. Click anywhere to report incidents or view existing reports.

🔄 Auto-refresh

Updates every 30 seconds to show the latest incidents in your area.

🔍 Smart Filters

Filter incidents by type to see only what matters to you.

📱 Mobile Ready

Responsive design works perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktop.

🎨 Color-coded

Quick visual identification with color-coded severity levels and incident types.
